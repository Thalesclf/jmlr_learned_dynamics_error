## Numbers quoted in the text

- `tables/paper_numbers.json` (written by `scripts/build_tables.py`): horizon summaries of the four surrogate families, Table 1, bootstrap intervals of the variance shares, the fine-tuning study and its attribution, the ensemble scores and the stress test of Appendix E (`pgd/l96_e2000/`).
- `tables/horizon_summary_*.csv` (written by `scripts/build_tables.py`): the values of Tables 5 to 9 at full precision.
- `tables/horizons_*.csv` and `tables/spectral_*.csv` (written by `python scripts/analysis.py l96_e20 l96_e150 l96_e2000 ks_pre`): medians of all window diagnostics and of the occupation of the singular spectrum at every horizon.
- Values read from a figure, such as the means over defect-tail pairs quoted in Section 6.4, are the plotted values saved in `data/processed/figure_data/`.
- `tangent/` (written by `scripts/run_tangent_check.py`): expansion of the learned tangent dynamics quoted in Section 6.8.
- `lyapunov_true_l96.json` and `lyapunov_true_ks.json` (written by `scripts/run_lyapunov.py`): leading Lyapunov exponents used to express the horizons in Lyapunov times.

## Plotted values

`scripts/export_matlab_plot_data.py` saves the values plotted in every figure in `data/processed/figure_data/`, as CSV files with named columns (one per figure, three for Figure 10). It computes them from the saved results as `scripts/plot_figures.py` does, and the files make the plotted values available without running the analysis. `scripts/generate_all_figures.m` draws the same figures in MATLAB from these files and saves them in `figures/matlab/`. It has not been run in MATLAB for this release, and the figures of the manuscript are those written by `scripts/plot_figures.py`. The MATLAB script of the first version of the study, which drew a different set of figures, is kept as `legacy/matlab/generate_all_figures_historical.m`.
